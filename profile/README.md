## Overview

Rocksmith connects guitar and bass practice with an interactive music game. Players follow note arrangements, perform musical passages, and receive feedback as they play. The experience can be adjusted to match different skill levels and practice goals.

The Remastered edition adds refinements to the original experience, including improved song management, customizable difficulty progression, expanded practice controls, and player statistics.

## Learning and Practice Workflow

Rocksmith is designed around active playing rather than passive instruction. Players connect an instrument, select an arrangement, and follow the on-screen notes while the game evaluates their performance.

A typical session can include:

1. Select a song or exercise suited to your current ability.
2. Play through the arrangement and observe the feedback.
3. Use Riff Repeater to isolate difficult sections.
4. Adjust the practice speed and difficulty where supported.
5. Repeat the passage and review your results.

This workflow makes it easier to focus on specific techniques instead of repeatedly struggling with an entire song.

## Practice Modes

### Learn a Song

Follow guitar or bass arrangements and develop familiarity with riffs, melodies, chords, and song structures. Adaptive difficulty can introduce more complex passages as playing improves.

### Riff Repeater

Focus on individual phrases and challenging transitions. The controls support targeted repetition, helping players work on difficult passages at a manageable pace.

### Guitarcade

Use short interactive challenges to practice skills such as note recognition, timing, and fretboard navigation.

### Session Mode

Experiment with musical ideas alongside virtual musicians. This mode offers an alternative to structured song practice and encourages exploration.

### Score Attack

Play with an emphasis on accuracy and performance consistency. Results provide an additional way to assess progress.

## Important Edition Notes

The original Rocksmith 2014 Remastered release and the later Learn & Play edition should not be treated as identical song libraries. The Learn & Play edition retains the core gameplay but uses a different selection of songs and exercises.

Check the edition name and included content before making decisions about installation or existing song libraries.
